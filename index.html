Linework

Direct MP3 Audio & CSV Fabrication Suite

📖 Information & Controls

Linework translates sound recordings or CSV point data into 3D linear sculptures formatted as continuous pipe runs or timber profiles.

World Orientation & Scaling

The lower-right XYZ indicator displays screen/world orientation (Red=X, Green=Y, Blue=Z). Scaling and 90° step rotations operate in world space in your active orientation.

Fusion 360 Centerline DXF Workflow

Export as a 3D DXF to import directly into Fusion 360 as a 3D Sketch path, allowing native 100% parametric Pipe or Sweep modeling.

Keyboard Shortcuts

  • O: Switch to Orbit Mode.
  • H: Switch to Pan Mode.
  • X / Y / Z: Rotate sculpture 90° along selected axis.
